We are Gaia and Gustavo – tango teachers, DJs and passionate travellers.

Since 2021, we have been organising Tango Escapes in different places across Germany. In summer, we also travel to other countries, combining our passion for tango with beautiful places, nature, good food and shared experiences.  
  
Gaia is from Rome, Italy, and has been living in Berlin since 2002. Gustavo is from Rosario, Argentina, and has been living in Berlin since 2018. Tango brought us together, and our shared love of travelling inspired us to create special trips for people who love tango as much as we do.

Our Tango Escapes in Germany usually last three or four days. Alongside our tango classes and milongas, we want to create a relaxed and personal atmosphere where people can dance, meet new friends and simply enjoy spending time together.

For some of our trips, we also cook for our participants. For us, good food and sharing a meal are just as much a part of a great Tango Escape as dancing itself.

In summer, we look for special destinations where you can enjoy much more than tango – places where you can relax by the sea, connect with nature, explore the surroundings and share excursions and experiences together.

**We love tango. We love travelling. And we love bringing the two together.**
